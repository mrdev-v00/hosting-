note one  ·  everyday magic

You make ordinary days feel like main character energy. Not the loud, movie-trailer kind — the quiet kind. The kind where we're both sprawled somewhere doing absolutely nothing, arguing about what to eat, and somehow that nothing becomes the thing I'd replay forever if I could. You turned boring car rides into comedy specials, chore time into gossip hour, and silence into something comfortable instead of empty. I honestly didn't know other people's "ordinary" was just ordinary. Mine comes with live commentary, dramatic sound effects, and someone stealing my fries without making eye contact.

note one  ·  continued

Somewhere between you "borrowing" my hoodies and me "borrowing" your chargers — between the 2 a.m. conversations that start with "quick question" and end two hours later with us questioning our entire existence — ordinary stopped existing. You made growing up feel like a two-player game, and I'm so glad we spawned on the same team. Out of everyone the universe could have handed me, it handed me you: a built-in best friend, a co-star in every embarrassing chapter, a permanent plus-one to my whole life. I've never once asked for a refund.

no cap, sleepyhead. best teammate I never even had to pick.

This certification is awarded for a lifetime of distinguished services: surviving my worst moods, guarding my deepest secrets, hyping me up when I needed it, and eating half my food without one shred of guilt. Position: permanent. Transferable: never. Refund policy: does not exist. Backed by absolutely zero official institutions and one extremely committed best friend who would like the fries back, but has accepted that this is the arrangement now.

notarized by me. valid forever.

note three  ·  the truth is

Behind all the teasing — I'm just really, really grateful you exist. Grateful for the version of you who bursts into my room to tell me something that could honestly have waited three days. For the one who remembers things I said once, in passing, and brings them up years later like evidence in court. For the one who knows exactly which silence means "leave me alone" and which one means "come sit with me and say nothing." You learned a whole language of me that nobody else ever studied — and you speak it fluently, without even trying.

note three  ·  continued

You're the best friend I ever stumbled into, the first person who taught me that family isn't just who you're born to — it's who you'd choose a hundred times out of a hundred. I'd choose you on my best day. I'd choose you on my worst one. I'd choose you in every universe where you exist, and honestly in a couple where you don't, just to prove a point.

don't let it get to your head though. it's already full. I checked.

note four  ·  the receipts

I keep receipts, you know. A whole private archive nobody can delete. Years of fighting over the TV remote like it was a family heirloom. The "borrowed" clothes that quietly migrated to your side of the closet and were never seen again. The time we broke the thing and stood there in silence like two lawyers agreeing on a story. Every shared plate, every split dessert where your half was somehow bigger. Every car ride where we went from arguing to laughing in under sixty seconds, and back again. Every inside joke so old by now that a single word is enough to take us both down.

note four  ·  continued

People say friendship is the purest kind of love — but they never had a best friend like you. They never had someone who has witnessed every single version of them: the embarrassing phases, the dramatic eras, the glow-ups, the breakdowns, the questionable hair decisions — and stayed through every one of them. Not because they had nowhere else to go, but because they never wanted to leave. That's not just friendship. That's witness. That's history. That's us — and I'd put the entire archive in a museum with one plaque on the wall: "proof that this one got impossibly lucky."

the archive is extensive. and still growing. forever.

note five  ·  unsaid things

We don't really do the sentimental thing, do we? We show love in insults, stolen snacks, and texting "wyd" at 2 a.m. We have never once finished a serious conversation without one of us wrecking it with a joke. We say "shut up" the way other people say "I missed you," and "you're so annoying" the way they say "I love you." I know we both understand the translation. But just this once — here, where you can't interrupt me — I want to say it in plain language, with no jokes loaded:

note five  ·  continued

You are my favorite notification. The first call for good news, bad news, and absolutely no news at all. The first audience for every joke, the first believer in every ridiculous plan, the first one standing in front of me when the world decides to be unfair. Being your best friend is the best thing I never had to work for — it just arrived the day you did, unearned and unbeatable, and it is still the best plot twist of my entire story.

don't screenshot this. (okay, fine. screenshot it. keep it forever.)

note six  ·  favorite distraction

You are the reason my phone screen time is off the charts. The first person I text when anything happens — good news, bad news, or just a random thought at 2 a.m. that could have waited. You're the human equivalent of a push notification I actually want to open. Even when you're being entirely too much, even when you're stealing my food or sending me 17 tiktoks in a row, I wouldn't trade a single second of it.

note six  ·  continued

Because the truth is, life was a lot quieter before you invaded it. You brought the noise, the drama, the constant pinging, and somehow made it the best soundtrack I could ask for. You're my favorite distraction, my built-in hype woman, and the only person who can make me laugh when I actively don't want to. You make it impossible to be bored, and honestly, I'm a little addicted to the chaos.

and I'd let you ruin my productivity all over again.

note seven  ·  the balance

They say best friends are the family you choose. Well, I chose you, and now I'm stuck with you. You're the one who knows all my secrets and uses them for blackmail (lovingly, of course). We cross lines, we push boundaries, we flirt with disaster on a daily basis — but at the end of the day, there's no one else I'd rather have in my corner. We know exactly how to drive each other crazy, and we do it with a smile.

note seven  ·  continued

You bring the chaos, I bring the dry sarcasm, and somehow it just works. We know exactly which buttons to push to drive each other insane. But we also know exactly when to stop, when to listen, and when to just sit in silence. That's a rare kind of balance. It's knowing someone so well that you can be a total menace around them, but also the person they trust with the soft, quiet parts of themselves.

don't worry, I won't get mushy. this is a one-time thing.

note eight  ·  late nights & deep talks

Behind the endless banter and the constant teasing, there are the 3 a.m. conversations. The ones where the jokes fade and we actually let our guards down. The ones where we talk about the things that scare us, the things we want, and the things we're too afraid to say in the daylight. Those are the moments that remind me this isn't just a surface-level friendship.

note eight  ·  continued

It's easy to be someone's friend when everything is fun. It's a lot harder to be the person they call when it's not. You've been that person for me more times than I can count, and I hope I've been that for you, too. You're the one I trust with the messy drafts of my life before I present them to the world. That's the real privilege of knowing you.

thanks for seeing the messy parts and sticking around.

Here's to another year of nonsense, naps, and nonstop laughing. To you sending me memes at 3 a.m. and me replying "lol" while actually laughing alone in the dark like a gremlin. To more half-finished jokes that no one else will ever decode. To your chaotic energy, your three-act storytelling, your criminal music taste, and your heart — which is somehow still bigger than your patience.

We'll probably still be doing exactly this in ten years — causing trouble, stealing each other's food, and pretending we're way cooler than we actually are. And honestly, that sounds like a pretty great deal to me. Here's to growing up, but never really growing apart.

ready for the next chapter, partner in crime?

When you shine, I'll be clapping the loudest and taking the most unflattering photos. When you fall, I'll already be there before you hit the ground — probably laughing, but there. That's not a promise I have to make. It's just the job. It came free with the title of "best friend."

I've got your back, today, tomorrow, and through every single sequel nobody asked for.

love you, troublemaker. happy birthday.
